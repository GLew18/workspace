WorkSpace · Focus End Sounds

The 10 completion cues that play when a focus session finishes. All are synthesized live with the Web Audio API — no audio files.

🎧 How to review: click Play on each one (turn your volume up a touch — they're intentionally gentle). Then tell Claude any edits per sound — e.g. "make Zen Gong longer," "Arcade is too harsh," "Bell needs more shimmer," "swap Ping for a softer chime." These are exactly the sounds wired into the app.

This is a throwaway review page (focus-sounds-review.html). The real sounds live in app/src/focus/sounds.ts and stay in sync with whatever edits you approve.

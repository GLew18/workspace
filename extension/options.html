Shortcuts are managed in the WorkSpace web app (the Links tab) and sync here automatically — there is nothing to configure on this page. A shortcut needs a modifier such as Alt, Ctrl, or ⌘. They fire on normal websites even when WorkSpace is in the background, but not on browser pages like chrome://, the New Tab page, the Web Store, the PDF viewer, or while you're typing in a text field. Browser- and OS-reserved combos (like Ctrl+T or Ctrl+W) can't be reassigned.
